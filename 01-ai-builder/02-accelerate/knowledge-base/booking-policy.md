# Harbour Dental Clinic — booking and cancellation policy

> Fictional business. Made up for a RAG exercise; not a real clinic.

## Booking

Appointments are booked through the front desk during opening hours, or through the online
booking page on our website at any time. New patients should allow an extra 10 minutes on their
first visit to fill in a medical history form.

We cannot book appointments over the voice assistant. It can answer questions and take a message
for the front desk to call back.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. There is no charge for
that.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and missed appointments, are charged a $40 late
cancellation fee. We waive it the first time.

## Running late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the appointment or rebook it, so
that patients after you are not kept waiting.

## Emergencies

We keep two emergency slots free every weekday, at 8:30 am and 1:30 pm, for severe toothache,
swelling, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth. Call as early in the day as possible to get one.

Outside opening hours, or for heavy bleeding, swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, or a
facial injury, go to the hospital emergency department.

## Children

Children under 16 must come with a parent or guardian. Children's check-ups are $60.

## Reminders

We send a text reminder two days before each appointment. Reply to it to confirm.
