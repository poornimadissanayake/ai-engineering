# Harbour Dental Clinic — clinic information

> Fictional business. Made up for a RAG exercise; not a real clinic, and none of this is dental
> advice.

## About us

Harbour Dental Clinic is a small family dental practice on Wharf Street, next to the ferry
terminal. We have two dentists, Dr Amara Lee and Dr Tomas Novak, and one dental hygienist,
Priya. We see adults and children from age three.

## Services and prices

Prices are for a standard appointment. The dentist confirms the final price before any treatment
starts.

| Service | Price | Length |
|---|---:|---|
| New patient exam (includes two X-rays) | $95 | 45 minutes |
| Check-up and clean (existing patients) | $120 | 45 minutes |
| Children's check-up (under 16) | $60 | 30 minutes |
| Hygienist clean only | $90 | 30 minutes |
| Small filling | $160 – $220 | 30–45 minutes |
| Teeth whitening (in chair) | $450 | 90 minutes |
| Emergency appointment | $80 assessment fee, plus any treatment | 30 minutes |

We do not offer braces, implants or wisdom-tooth surgery. For those we refer patients to a
specialist.

## Payment

We take card and bank transfer. Payment is due on the day of the appointment. We do not take
cash. We can give a printed or emailed receipt for health-insurance claims, but we do not claim
directly from insurers.

## Location and parking

We are on the ground floor, with step-free access. There are two free patient parking spaces
behind the building; otherwise the ferry terminal car park is a two-minute walk.
